# Tally rules (Ryan, 2026-10-04)

- Ryan's score counts 1.5x in the combined average; every other rater counts 1x.
- Ratings out of 10 are halved to the /5 scale.
- A style's score per rater = the average of that rater's per-picture ratings for the style
  (a style-level rating stands in for any picture they did not rate individually).
- Unrated styles are left out for that rater, not counted as zero.
